It started with friends asking for more.

Doce Dough started the way most good bakeries do — with a home oven, a full sheet pan, and friends circling back for seconds. We built our menu around the treats we grew up on: American-style cookies and muffins on one side, and traditional Brazilian brigadeiros on the other.

Every platter is made in small batches in Broward County, the same week as your event. No warehouses, no shortcuts — just recipes we'd serve at our own family's table, packed up and ready for yours.

Three recipes, perfected — mix and match to build a dessert table that covers every guest. Platters scale from small family gatherings to full office events.

Crowd favorite

Chocolate Chip Cookies

Classic, golden-brown cookies with crisp edges and soft, chewy centers, loaded generously with chocolate chips. Baked fresh for your event, they're the platter that empties first at every party.

Great for: birthdays, office breakrooms, holiday tables
Brunch & dessert

Chocolate Chip Banana Muffins

Moist, flavorful muffins made with ripe bananas and studded with chocolate chips in every bite. Enjoyable at home on a brunch spread or stacked alongside a dessert table.

Great for: brunches, baby showers, morning meetings
Brazilian tradition

Traditional Chocolate Brigadeiros

Rich, authentic Brazilian chocolate truffles, slow-stirred until dense and fudgy, hand-rolled and finished in chocolate sprinkles. A staple of Brazilian celebrations, made the traditional way.

Great for: milestone birthdays, quinceañeras, holiday gifting
